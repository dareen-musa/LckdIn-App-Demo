## Brand & Style

This design system serves an intentional, mobile-first opportunity discovery and project collaboration network for university students. The interface deliberately diverges from gamified swiping tropes, casino-like micro-rewards, and noisy social feeds. Instead, it embraces an editorial minimalism inspired by high-end publication layouts and curated knowledge apps.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on:
- **Calm Intentionality:** Unhurried, breathable spaces that treat career development, co-founder search, and research matching as substantial life milestones rather than transactional card flips.
- **Editorial Substance:** High-contrast typography paired with creamy organic surfaces, evoking premium print journals and bespoke academic digests.
- **Dignified Interactions:** Fluid gesture-based physics, smooth micro-elevations, and understated feedback that respect user focus without flashing badges or artificial urgency counters.

## Colors

The palette establishes an academic yet contemporary atmosphere, anchoring energetic student pursuits in grounded, archival hues.

- **Primary (`#4C050D` - Deep Rich Burgundy):** The core authoritative tone, applied to dominant brand display typography, key headers, active structural frames, and primary anchor items.
- **Secondary (`#8C0610` - Vibrant Crimson):** The primary interaction and action color. Reserved strictly for forward movement, high-intent primary buttons, vital notifications, and dynamic matching affordances.
- **Tertiary (`#95B1C7` - Gentle Slate Blue):** A serene counterweight used for supportive project categories, meta status indications, soft focus outlines, and secondary tag containers.
- **Surface & Canvas Foundations:**
  - Base Canvas: `#FBFBF9` (Off-white / Warm Rice Paper).
  - Elevated Container / Secondary Canvas: `#F4F2EC` (Light Sand).
  - Subtle Border Accent / Neutral Divider: `#E2DFCE` (Warm Beige).
- **Text & Contrast Hierarchy:**
  - High-emphasis text: `#1C1917` (Deep Ink Charcoal) and `#4C050D` (Burgundy for Titles).
  - Medium-emphasis text: `#57534E` (Muted Warm Slate).
  - Low-emphasis text & subtle metadata: `#8C877E` (Field Grey).

## Typography

The type system relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve a balance between geometric clarity and editorial warmth. Its open counters and clean geometry ensure readability in dense opportunity cards while maintaining an authoritative tone in editorial display titles.

- **Editorial Headings:** Large display instances use tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`700`–`800`) paired with the primary burgundy color to command the canvas like an editorial front page.
- **Reading Comfort:** Body copy leverages comfortable line-heights (`1.55`–`1.65`) on top of soft neutral backgrounds to reduce optical fatigue during detailed opportunity reading.
- **Categorical Tags:** Micro labels and tags employ uppercase or semi-bold styling with subtle positive letter tracking (`+0.03em` to `+0.05em`) for immediate recognition at small scales.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored by a mobile-first, single-column viewport architecture designed specifically for gesture discovery, scaling upward into a disciplined multi-column editorial grid for wider displays.

- **Mobile Viewport (Base to 639px):** 
  - Standard edge margin of `1.25rem` (`20px`) ensuring edge-to-edge immersion for discovery cards while keeping content within thumb reach.
  - Card discovery views allocate a maximum card canvas ratio of roughly `4:5`, preserving vertical breathing room for top filters and bottom dock navigators.
- **Tablet / Large Handheld (640px to 1023px):**
  - Margins expand to `2rem`. The discovery interface settles into a constrained central column (max-width `540px`) flanked by ancillary context panels or split-screen matching queues.
- **Desktop (1024px+):**
  - 12-column grid with `2rem` gutters and `3rem` margins. The primary discovery stage retains a focused editorial aspect ratio centered on screen, flanked by project dossiers and live applicant collaboration boards.
- **Rhythm & Gaps:** All spacing adheres strictly to a 4px/8px incremental cadence. Micro gaps (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) structure tag groups and icon-text locks; generous tokens (`space-lg`, `space-xl`) isolate distinct narrative sections within candidate and project briefs.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than layered synthetic drop-shadows or stark borders, depth is communicated through a blend of physical card layering and warm, diffuse ambient illumination.

- **Card Resting Depth:** The primary opportunity swipe cards rest on a multi-stage ambient shadow tinted with the primary burgundy pigment:
  - Ambient: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(76, 5, 13, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.04)`.
  - Rim Outline: 1px structural stroke using `#E2DFCE` to preserve boundary clarity against the `#FBFBF9` canvas.
- **Card Interactive / Drag Depth:** When a card is engaged via touch or hover, the elevation softly blooms:
  - Active: `0 24px 48px -6px rgba(76, 5, 13, 0.12), 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.06)`.
  - Transform: Slight scale lift of `1.015` accompanied by smooth spring physics.
- **Receded Containers (Deck Stacking):** Cards beneath the active candidate card scale progressively downward (`0.95`, `0.90`) and recede in opacity (`0.7`, `0.4`), layered in `#F4F2EC` surfaces without individual drop shadows.
- **Floating Controls & Bottom Dock:** Navigation bars and floating micro-action dials employ a frosted editorial backdrop (`rgba(251, 251, 249, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) paired with a razor-thin border (`#E2DFCE`).

## Shapes

The design system employs an organic, high-radius curvature language that reinforces a welcoming and tactile feel throughout the app.

- **Core Discovery Cards:** Utilize `rounded-3xl` (`2rem` / `32px`) on outer container perimeters, giving opportunity decks the character of smooth physical stock boards.
- **Action Triggers & Chips:** Buttons, meta tags, and interactive chips adopt fully continuous pill radii (`rounded-full`), preventing harsh visual edges and prioritizing finger-friendly targets.
- **Inner Content Blocks & Modals:** Nested internal elements (such as code snippets, author callouts, or requirement modules) use `rounded-2xl` (`1.25rem` / `20px`) to maintain harmonious radius nesting inside parent containers.

## Components

### Opportunity Discovery Cards
- **Structure:** Encased in a `rounded-3xl` frame with `#FBFBF9` background and a `#E2DFCE` structural border. Divided into an editorial masthead (role title, project or lab name, university department) and a content body detailing the challenge, stack, and commitment.
- **Micro-Indicators:** Header contains an unobtrusive tactile bookmark cue and an eligibility badge.
- **Swipe Physics Cue:** Neutral visual affordances—subtle gradient glows at the card edges: faint `#8C0610` tint along the right edge for "Express Interest" and soft `#95B1C7` tint along the left for "Pass/Archive".

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Connect / Apply):** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#8C0610` fill with crisp white text. Elevated on hover/press with gentle depth bloom. Height is 48px to 52px for mobile ergonomics.
- **Secondary Action (Bookmark / Share):** Pill-shaped, `#F4F2EC` background, `#4C050D` label, with a delicate border in `#E2DFCE`.
- **Swipe Auxiliary Dials:** Floating circular buttons (`56px × 56px`) at the bottom of the discovery canvas for non-gesture fallback: Pass (Slate border, `#FBFBF9` fill) and Connect (Crimson fill, white glyph).

### Chips & Refined Pill Tags
- **Category & Discipline Tags:** Compact height (28px to 32px), pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), text set in `label-md`. 
  - *Engineering & Tech:* Background `rgba(149, 177, 199, 0.20)`, text `#2B4A60`.
  - *Research & Academia:* Background `rgba(76, 5, 13, 0.08)`, text `#4C050D`.
  - *Skill Micro-Pills:* Background `#F4F2EC`, border `1px solid #E2DFCE`, text `#57534E`.

### Minimalist Navigation Dock
- **Layout:** Floating mobile navigation dock suspended `1.25rem` above the bottom screen safe area.
- **Styling:** Frosted translucency (`rgba(251, 251, 249, 0.88)` with `blur(20px)`), `rounded-full` contour, containing three to four minimalist icon glyphs.
- **Active State:** Deep burgundy `#4C050D` glyph with a warm crimson dot beneath; inactive states rendered in muted `#8C877E`.

### Inputs & Discovery Filter Controls
- **Search & Query Fields:** Soft `#F4F2EC` background, no hard borders until focused. On focus, transitions to a 1.5px `#95B1C7` outline with zero exterior harsh glow.
- **Checkboxes & Multi-Select:** Generous `24px` rounded squares (`rounded-md`) with custom checkmarks in `#8C0610` when active.
